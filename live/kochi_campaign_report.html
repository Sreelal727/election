The GreyCell Sentinel monitors 10 digital platforms continuously, aggregating coverage volume, sentiment polarity, and engagement metrics into a single Composite Media Sentiment Index (0–100 scale). The last three days of the campaign showed the following pattern:

4.1 April 5 — The Surge

Peak Index: 94.7 at 17:18 IST — the highest reading of the entire campaign.

The day began at a baseline of 8–12 (overnight). By 10:30, the morning news cycle lifted it to ~32. A brief dip at 11:00 was followed by a sharp spike past 45 at 11:30. The first peak of ~62 arrived at 13:00. After a post-peak correction to ~48, a second massive surge at 14:17 drove the index to its all-time high of 94.7 at 17:18. Data collection ended at 17:39 with the index still at 89.3.

Impact: +847% surge from morning baseline. All monitored platforms showed concurrent peak activity. UDF coverage dominated with a sentiment weight of +0.57 pct points — the highest single adjustment of the campaign.

4.2 April 6 — Sustained but Lower

Peak Index: 48 — Reached during the afternoon news cycle.

The April 5 surge generated residual momentum. Coverage remained elevated but the intensity dropped by roughly half. The index fluctuated between 25–48 during active hours. Overnight baseline returned to ~10. The UDF sentiment adjustment held at +0.28, indicating continued positive coverage but without a new catalyst.

4.3 April 7 — Final Stretch Momentum

The index pattern showed a more sustained elevation (35–54 range for 6+ hours) compared to April 6’s sharper peak-and-fade. This suggests broader, distributed coverage rather than a single viral event. Multiple platforms showed concurrent positive UDF activity. LDF’s media counter-offensive failed to register above background noise.

A.2 Prediction Engine — Multi-Layer Convergence Architecture

  • Bayesian Stochastic Convergence Engine (BSCE): 4,700,000 convergence trials per constituency with adaptive variance sampling. Each trial perturbs vote share distributions through Gaussian priors calibrated against 4 election cycles. 750,000 rapid-convergence trials used for hourly live updates.
  • Hierarchical Swing Transfer Model (HSTM): Multi-level dampening framework — LSG ward-level results mapped through a non-linear transfer function (validated at 0.45 dampening coefficient against 2016→2021 actuals across 140 constituencies).
  • Adaptive Volatility Kernel: Base dispersion of 4.0 pct + margin-proximity factor + historical hand-change bonus. The kernel auto-adjusts based on cross-election correlation coefficients (2016, 2021, 2024).
  • Real-Time Sentiment Fusion Layer (RTSFL): Front-weighted bilingual keyword analysis across 10 digital platforms, processed through a temporal decay function with 6-hour half-life. Adjustments capped at ±1.5 pct points. Feed refresh: every 30 minutes.
  • Correlated Aggregate Projection: 50,000 joint simulations across all 140 constituencies with inter-constituency correlation modelling for state-level seat projections.
  • Ensemble Confidence Scoring: Final predictions are the convergence point of 3 independent model layers (swing-based, booth-projection, sentiment-adjusted), cross-validated against 2024 Parliament results as out-of-sample check.
