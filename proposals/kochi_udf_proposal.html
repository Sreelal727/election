1. Executive Summary

Kerala goes to polls on April 9, 2026 — just 9 days from today. The Kochi constituency presents an unprecedented electoral landscape. For the first time, this is a genuine three-cornered contest. Twenty20's alliance with the NDA has transformed a traditional UDF-LDF battleground into a complex multi-front arena where 64,996 votes are in active play across the swing pool.

Macro-level indicators favour UDF — a +25.54% swing in the 2025 LSG elections, 95 out of 147 wards won, and strong anti-incumbency against the 10-year LDF government. But elections are not won on macro indicators. The margin between a comfortable victory and an uncomfortably tight finish depends on how precisely the campaign responds to ground-level shifts in the next 9 days. Every day of inaction is a day the opposition uses to consolidate.

Our Proposition: We deploy immediately — a full-spectrum election intelligence and strategy operation combining classified data collection, real-time sentiment tracking, AI-driven analysis, PR strategy, and ground-team guidance. A dedicated 6-person team and a live campaign dashboard, operational from Day 1, giving your campaign the information advantage to convert favourable conditions into a decisive mandate before April 9.

3. Scope of Services

We offer nine integrated service modules that together form a complete election intelligence apparatus. Each module feeds into the others, creating a data loop where every ground observation, social media signal, and voter interaction sharpens the campaign's strategic response.

3.1 Data Collection & Segregation from Classified Sources

We deploy a multi-layered data collection framework that aggregates intelligence from sources not accessible through conventional polling or public data.

  • Electoral Roll Analysis: Voter-level data from the 240-part SIR (State Integrated Result) covering all 1,51,376 electors — gender ratios, new voter registrations, deletions, and migration patterns, extracted through OCR from ECI PDFs.
  • Booth-Level Historical Data: Granular 2021 Assembly + 2024 Parliament results for all 157 booths, enabling booth-by-booth swing mapping and vote-transfer modelling.
  • Ward-Level LSG Intelligence: 2020 and 2025 Local Self-Government results across 147 wards from 5 local bodies (Kochi Corporation, Palluruthy, Chellanam, Kumbalangi, Mulavukadu), providing the most recent voter mood indicator.
  • Community and Demographic Profiling: Booth-level community composition mapping (Latin Catholic, Muslim, Hindu OBC/Ezhava, Fishermen, etc.) cross-referenced with voting patterns.
  • Rival Campaign Intelligence: Systematic tracking of LDF and NDA campaign activities, rally attendance, and messaging strategy across all five zones.

All data is segregated by zone, booth category (A through F), community segment, and risk level — giving campaign managers actionable micro-targeted intelligence rather than raw numbers.

3.2 PR Analysis

Continuous monitoring and analysis of the constituency's public relations landscape:

  • Digital Media Monitoring: Daily tracking of Kochi constituency coverage across digital news portals, YouTube news channels, online editions of Malayalam media, political blogs, and social media commentary.
  • Narrative Mapping: Identification of dominant narratives — which issues are being framed by which front, which narratives are gaining traction, and which counter-narratives need deployment.
  • Candidate Perception Audit: Weekly assessment of how Mohammad Shiyas (UDF), K.J. Maxi (LDF), and Xavier Joolappan (NDA) are being perceived across media and public discourse — likability, visibility, credibility, and issue ownership.
  • Competitor PR Tracking: Analysis of LDF and NDA's PR spends, influencer tie-ups, and messaging frequency to identify their strategic priorities and vulnerabilities.
  • Issue Salience Tracking: Real-time ranking of which issues matter most to voters this week (Brahmapuram, FIDF, Metro, development, corruption) and how salience shifts over time.

3.3 PR Strategy

Beyond analysis, we provide actionable PR strategy recommendations tailored to the Kochi constituency's unique dynamics:

  • Zone-Specific Messaging Frameworks: Different messages for different zones — coastal development for Chellanam/Kumbalangi, urban governance for Fort Kochi, welfare and Catholic community engagement for Chullickal-Nazareth.
  • Counter-Narrative Playbooks: Pre-prepared responses to anticipated LDF attacks (e.g., solar scam references, Congress legacy criticisms) and NDA provocations (communal framing, development comparisons).
  • Daily PR Action Plans: Specific, time-bound recommendations each morning — which events to hold today, which media appearances to schedule, which social media campaigns to push, and which local leaders to activate in which booths.
  • Crisis Communication Protocols: Rapid-response frameworks for handling negative coverage, rival attacks, viral misinformation, or unexpected events (candidate gaffes, controversy, etc.).
  • Influencer & Opinion Maker Strategy: Identification and engagement plan for key local opinion makers — parish priests, masjid committees, trade union leaders, resident association heads, local media personalities — mapped to their sphere of booth-level influence.

Classified Intelligence — Darkweb Forum Monitoring

HIGHLY CONFIDENTIAL

In addition to public social media platforms, we monitor classified darkweb forums and encrypted channels where political operatives, rival campaign insiders, and underground networks discuss election strategy, booth-level manipulation plans, vote-buying operations, and communal mobilisation tactics. This intelligence is derived, anonymised, and delivered as a separate confidential briefing — never mixed into the public sentiment dashboard. No weightage is assigned to this data in the prediction model; it serves purely as strategic intelligence for campaign leadership's situational awareness and counter-preparedness.

Access to darkweb intelligence reports is restricted to the Chief Strategist and designated campaign leadership only. These reports are delivered in person or via encrypted channels and are not stored on any shared system.

Sentiment Shift Detection

More valuable than a one-time score is the ability to detect shifts — a candidate trending downward, an issue suddenly gaining traction, or a rival's messaging beginning to resonate. Our system generates alerts when:

  • Any front's sentiment moves more than ±0.15 within a 72-hour window
  • A new issue enters the top-3 salience ranking
  • SDPI-related mentions exceed a threshold in Kochi context (split risk indicator)
  • NDA momentum in Chellanam booths rises above consolidation threshold

3.5 Ground Data Analysis

We process and analyse ground-level intelligence that campaign workers collect daily:

  • Booth-Level Mood Tracking: Standardised reporting templates for booth agents — voter inclination, issue priorities, candidate recognition, rival activity. Aggregated daily into zone-level assessments.
  • Rally & Event Impact Analysis: Post-event assessment of rallies, road shows, and door-to-door campaigns — attendance numbers, crowd sentiment, media pickup, and estimated voter conversion.
  • Community Leader Feedback Loop: Structured input from parish committees, masjid leaders, trade unions, and fishermen cooperatives — qualitative intelligence that no social media platform captures.
  • Voter Grievance Mapping: Systematic cataloguing of voter complaints and demands by booth, enabling the campaign to address hyperlocal issues (a broken road in booth 76, water supply in booth 23) that win individual voter loyalty.

3.6 Derivative Data & Analysis

Beyond raw data, we generate derivative intelligence products that reveal patterns invisible in surface-level numbers:

  • Vote Transfer Modelling: Where exactly are LDF's lost 24,000 votes going? Our booth-level models decompose the flow: anti-incumbency to UDF, BDJS defection to NDA, T20 redistribution, and turnout-driven changes.
  • Cross-Election Correlation Analysis: Mapping how 2025 LSG ward results predict 2026 Assembly outcomes at the booth level — which wards are reliable predictors and which are outliers requiring separate treatment.
  • Monte Carlo Win Probability: 1,000,000-simulation Monte Carlo model incorporating all data layers — base results, district swing, constituency swing, booth projections, and sentiment — producing a continuously updated win probability with confidence intervals.
  • Scenario Modelling: "What if SDPI contests?" "What if turnout drops 3% in Catholic belt?" "What if NDA consolidates all T20 booths?" — each scenario's impact on the final margin, computed and refreshed every 2 days as new ground data comes in.
  • Volatility Indexing: Every booth classified by volatility (Low/Moderate/High/Extreme) based on historical swing magnitude, margin thinness, three-way contest dynamics, and community composition — enabling intelligent resource allocation.

3.8 Ground-Work Team Guidance

Data is only valuable when it reaches the people making voter contact. We provide:

  • Daily Booth-Level Briefs: Every morning, booth agents receive a one-page brief: which households to prioritise today, which issues to lead with, which rival narratives to counter, and what intelligence to collect.
  • Zone Coordinator Dashboards: Each zone coordinator gets a simplified dashboard showing their zone's status — booths on track, booths at risk, today's priorities, and comparison with targets.
  • Talking Points by Community: Customised messaging guides for different community interactions — what to say in a Catholic household vs. a Muslim household vs. a fishermen household vs. an urban apartment complex.
  • Feedback Mechanism: A simple reporting system (WhatsApp-based or app-based) where booth workers submit daily observations that feed directly into our analysis pipeline.
  • Daily Strategy Syncs: Structured daily briefings with campaign leadership — what the data says today, what shifted overnight, what needs to change, and recommended course corrections for the next 24 hours.

All of the above converges into a single, live dashboard accessible to campaign leadership:

  • Constituency Overview: Current win probability, projected vote shares, margin estimates with confidence bands
  • Zone-by-Zone Status: Heat map showing which zones are on track, which are slipping, and which are outperforming
  • Booth-Level Drill-Down: Click any booth to see its historical data, current swing estimate, volatility index, and ground worker observations
  • Sentiment Tracker: Live sentiment scores per platform with 30-day trend lines, shift alerts, and issue salience rankings
  • Swing Monitor: Real-time tracking of the 64,996 swing pool — which segments are consolidating for UDF, which are drifting to rivals
  • PR Impact View: Media coverage tracker, narrative dominance meter, and social media engagement metrics
  • Risk Dashboard: Active risk factors (SDPI split probability, turnout forecast, NDA consolidation in Chellanam) with severity ratings

Access: Web-based dashboard accessible from any device. Secure login for authorised campaign personnel. Data updated every 6 hours from Day 1, escalating to every 2 hours from Apr 6 onwards, and hourly on E-Day (Apr 9).

9.2 Data Privacy & Ethics

  • All data collection is conducted through publicly available sources and legitimate channels.
  • No personal voter data is collected, stored, or used beyond what is available in the public electoral roll.
  • Social media monitoring is limited to publicly posted content — no private messages, closed groups, or protected accounts are accessed.
  • All analysis is objective and data-driven — we report what the data shows, not what the client wants to hear.

9.3 Scope Boundaries

  • This proposal covers the Kochi constituency (#80) only. Extension to additional constituencies can be discussed separately.
  • We provide intelligence and strategic recommendations. Campaign execution (voter contact, events, media buys) remains the responsibility of the campaign team.
  • Predictions and projections are based on the best available data and rigorous methodology. They represent probabilistic assessments, not guarantees of electoral outcomes.
