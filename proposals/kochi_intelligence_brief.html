WhatsApp Intelligence — Active Disinformation Detected

We are currently monitoring 11 constituency-level WhatsApp groups where coordinated communal messaging against the UDF candidate is being circulated. Kochi is a constituency where both the LDF and T20–NDA candidates are Christian, while the UDF candidate is Muslim. This demographic reality is being weaponised — the content being circulated frames the election as a religious identity choice, urges the Latin Catholic and Syro-Malabar communities to "vote for their own," and positions the UDF candidate as an outsider to the constituency's majority Christian electorate. The messaging is calibrated, not crude — it avoids overt hate speech but systematically seeds doubt about community representation. This is designed to suppress UDF turnout in the Catholic belt (Chullickal, Kumbalangi, Fort Kochi) and fracture the UDF coalition at its base. Separately, a parallel campaign targeting the T20–NDA alliance is being run — framing them as "BJP stooges selling out the fishing community" — which, while damaging to NDA, is also creating a generalised cynicism that depresses overall turnout (which hurts UDF more than LDF in this constituency).

We are factoring this disinformation velocity into our sentiment decay model. The 4.9% erosion rate already accounts for WhatsApp-driven narrative damage. Counter-messaging in these groups — or activating parallel UDF-aligned groups — could slow or reverse the erosion by 1.5–2.0% per cycle.

We have identified 12 specific booths where targeted ground pressure in the final 7 days can recover 4,500 – 7,200 votes, reversing the erosion and pushing the margin beyond LDF's recovery capacity. These were selected from 157 booths using three filters: (1) High count of persuadable voters — floating T20 voters, soft anti-incumbency defectors, volatile swing blocks; (2) Extreme volatility — proven history of large-block voter movement under pressure; (3) Actionable gap — booths where UDF leads are thin enough to lose or where a push converts a trailing position into a lead.
